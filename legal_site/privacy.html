Privacy Policy

Transit Pro · Last updated 20 November 2026

Transit Pro connects families with school, college and university van drivers: families find and book a driver, follow the van live during a trip, and pay the driver; drivers plan and run their rounds. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

Transit Pro is operated by Transit Pro. Contact: talhaaslamdev@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information — your name, email address, phone number, account type (parent, student or driver), profile photo, and sign-in details if you sign in with Google.
Students and children — for each student: name, institute, grade or semester, student ID number, photo, home pickup location, institute drop-off location, weekly school and pickup timings, days marked as not riding, and any notes the family adds. A parent enters this for their child; a student may enter it for themselves.
Emergency contacts — names, relationships and phone numbers a family adds, and the family's own name and phone, so the driver can reach someone if a student cannot be contacted.
Driver information — name, phone, driving licence number, years of experience, identity and licence document images (for verification), vehicle details and seat capacity, the institutes and areas served, rounds and timings, starting point, and the bank or mobile-wallet details the driver chooses to show to families for payment.
Location — while a driver has started a route, the driver's phone shares its precise location so families on that round can see the van and arrival times. This happens only during an active trip and is shown by an ongoing notification. Families and students may use their location when they choose to pin a place on the map. The app does not collect location in the background outside an active trip.
Trips and attendance — trips run, the stops and route of each trip, and whether each student was picked up, dropped off or absent.
Bookings, fees and payments — seat requests, the fee agreed with the driver, bills, payment records, the payment method a family names, and screenshots of payments a family uploads as proof. The app does not process card payments or move money.
Automatic checks of screenshots and documents — when a family attaches a payment screenshot, or a driver adds a photo of their driving licence or vehicle registration card, the app reads the text in the image on the phone itself to warn about likely mistakes (a different amount, the wrong receiver, an old or reused screenshot, an expired licence, a vehicle that does not match). Only the result is kept with the payment or document: the amount, transaction ID, date, receiver and sender names, the last four digits of an account number, and for documents the name, expiry date, licence categories and vehicle details shown on it (a CNIC only as its last four digits). The full text read from the image is not stored. These checks only warn; a driver still confirms every payment and an administrator still verifies every document.
Messages and notifications — chat messages between families and drivers, in-app notifications, and a device token used to deliver push notifications.
Ratings — ratings families give drivers.

How we use it

  • To match families with drivers and manage seat bookings and fees.
  • To plan routes, show the van's live location and estimated arrival times, and record attendance.
  • To send notifications about pickups, drop-offs, bookings, fees and messages.
  • To verify drivers before they can accept students.
  • To check payment screenshots and driver documents for likely mistakes, and warn the family, driver or administrator.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell personal information and do not show advertising.

Who can see your information

  • Your driver or your families — a driver sees the details, locations, timings and emergency contacts of the students booked with them, and of students who send them a seat request. Families see their driver's profile, vehicle and payment details and, during a trip on their child's round, the van's location.
  • Administrators of Transit Pro, to verify drivers and resolve problems.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app on our behalf: Google Firebase (accounts, database, notifications), Google Maps Platform (maps, routes, place search), Cloudinary (image storage), Cloudflare (notification, route and image-reading delivery), Google ML Kit (reads the text in a payment screenshot or document photo on your phone; the image does not leave the device for this) and Google Gemini (reads a payment screenshot or document image only when the on-phone reading fails; the image is sent through our Cloudflare service, used only to return the reading, and not stored by us; under Google's terms for its free tier, Google may use such images to improve its services). They process data only to provide these services.
  • Where required by law.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). Access is restricted by security rules so each person can read only the records their role needs.

Retention and deletion

Your information is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from the app (Profile → Delete Account) or by following the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting an account removes your profile and the records of your children or students; some records may be kept for a limited time where needed for security, disputes or legal reasons.

Children

Transit Pro is intended for parents and drivers aged 18 and over, and for students aged 16 and over. Information about younger children is entered and managed by their parent; they do not use the app themselves. If you believe a child's information was provided without a parent's consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Update your details in the app at any time.
  • Turn location or notifications off in your phone's settings (live tracking and alerts will then not work).
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, at talhaaslamdev@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page if this policy changes, and change the date at the top. See also our Terms of Service.
