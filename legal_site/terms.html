1. What Transit Pro is

Transit Pro is a platform that helps families find independent van drivers for school, college and university transport, book seats, follow the van during a trip, and keep a record of fees. The transport itself is provided by the driver, not by Transit Pro. Transit Pro is not a transport operator and does not employ drivers.

5. Families and students
