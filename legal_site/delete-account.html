Delete your Transit Pro account

Transit Pro

From the app

  1. Open Transit Pro and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Without the app

Email talhaaslamdev@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject Delete my Transit Pro account. We will confirm and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile, phone number and photo.
  • Your children's or student records, locations, timings and emergency contacts.
  • Your messages, notifications and push tokens.
  • For drivers: profile, vehicle, documents and payment details.

What may be kept

Trip, attendance and payment records that involve another person (for example a driver's record of fees received) may be kept for up to 12 months for disputes and record-keeping, without your contact details, and then deleted.
