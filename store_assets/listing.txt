SHORT DESCRIPTION
Track your school van live, find verified drivers and manage monthly fees.

FULL DESCRIPTION
Transit Pro makes daily school, college and university van transport simple and safe for families, students and drivers.

FOR PARENTS
• Find a verified van driver who serves your child's school, college or university
• Send a seat request for each child and agree the monthly fee in the app
• Follow the van live on the map during pickup and drop-off
• See expected pickup and drop-off times for your child
• Get notifications when the van is about to arrive, when your child is picked up, and when they are dropped off
• Mark the days your child is not riding, so the driver skips your stop
• Keep track of monthly bills, upload payment proof and see what is paid, pending or overdue
• Chat with your driver and keep emergency contacts up to date

FOR STUDENTS (16+)
• Manage your own pickup and drop-off locations, timings and institute details
• Track your van live and see your round, schedule and trip history

FOR DRIVERS
• Create morning and afternoon rounds with your own timings and seat limits
• Get seat requests from families nearby and accept the ones that fit your route
• Start a route and let families follow it live; the stops are put in a sensible order for you
• Mark pickup, drop-off and absence for each student
• Send monthly bills, reminders and confirm payments received
• Show your bank or mobile-wallet details so families can pay you directly

PRIVACY AND SAFETY
• Drivers are verified by our team before they can accept students
• The van's location is shared only while the driver has started a route, and only with families on that round
• Each family sees only its own child's details
• Transit Pro does not show ads and does not handle money; families pay drivers directly
